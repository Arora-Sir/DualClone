# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, background, identity, experience level or nationality.

## Expected behavior

* Be kind and patient with people who are learning.
* Give and accept constructive feedback.
* Stay on topic and assume good intent.
* Respect other people's time and privacy. Do not post anyone's personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

* Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
* Trolling, spam or repeated off-topic posts.
* Publishing private information without permission.
* Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. Report a problem privately through a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/Arora-Sir/DualClone/security/advisories/new) marked as a conduct report, or by contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, its issues, discussions and pull requests, and anywhere you represent the project.

This text is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
